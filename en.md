# Privacy Policy for The Waiting Friend

**Last updated: September 17, 2026**
**Publisher: Youkar.** Contact: YoukarC@hotmail.com

> This text accurately describes what the app does. It is not legal advice.

## In one sentence

The Waiting Friend runs entirely on your phone: no account, no server, no
internet connection. Nothing that happens in it can leave your device.

## What stays on your device

Everything the game records is game data, produced by playing, and nothing
else: the time and duration of your visits, the gestures and actions you
perform in the room (feeding, gifting, petting, playing, moving an object…),
the companion's journal entries, your answers to the in-game questions, the
creature's appearance and state, and your settings (notifications, sound,
content warning accepted, the optional Last Word notification on or off).

All of this is stored in a local database (SQLite), inside the app's private
storage, which other apps cannot access. Uninstalling the game erases it
permanently; an in-game reset has the same effect without uninstalling.

## What leaves your device

**Nothing.** The app does not request internet access permission: it is
technically unable to send any data anywhere, to any service, at any time.

## Advertising

No advertising. No advertising identifier is requested or used.

## In-app purchases

No in-app purchases. The Waiting Friend is a paid game, bought once on
Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the publisher never
sees or receives any billing or payment data.

## Permissions, and why

- **Notifications** — optional, off by default: at most one gentle reminder
  per day, never between 10 PM and 8 AM, and can be turned off in one tap
  from the bell on the main screen.
- **Boot** — reschedule an already-accepted notification after the phone
  restarts; without it, the notification would be lost.
- **Vibration** — haptic feedback for notifications and some in-game
  gestures.

The Waiting Friend requests **no access** to your location, contacts,
photos, camera, microphone, calendar, or the list of apps on your phone.

## What the app does not do

There is **no account** to create: no email address, no password, no online
profile. Nothing identifies you.

There is **no analytics**, no measurement tool, no automatic crash
reporting, no third-party component observing your usage. The app has no
permission to access the internet, so none of this is even possible.

Everything the creature "knows" about you comes exclusively from what you do
in the game (opening times, duration, gestures) — never from your contacts,
photos, location, or any other real data on your phone.

## Children

The Waiting Friend is intended for an adult audience; it deals with
psychological themes (obsession, possessiveness, jealousy) disclosed on the
store listing and at first launch. It does not collect any data, regardless
of the age of the person playing.

## Your rights

GDPR gives you a right of access, rectification, erasure and portability
over your data. Here, these rights are exercised directly inside the app,
with no need to write to us and no delay:

- **Access**: the creature's journal, profile and records show what the
  game has recorded of your visits and gestures.
- **Erasure**: the reset option in the game's settings, or uninstalling the
  app, deletes all data.

We hold no copy of your data: we could neither send it to you nor delete it
on your behalf, since we never have it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page is updated. Since
the app cannot reach you, this is where to check.
